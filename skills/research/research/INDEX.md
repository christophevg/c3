### Relevant and Tight Testing

**Folder**: `2026-07-06-relevant-tight-testing/`
**Date**: 2026-07-06
**Status**: Complete

**Summary**: Comprehensive guide on Python unit testing best practices with focus on "relevant and tight" testing - reducing test bloat while maintaining quality by testing behavior over implementation. Covers what to test vs what NOT to test, testing philosophy from Kent Beck and Martin Fowler, test quality indicators, anti-patterns, and practical decision trees.

**Key Findings**:
- Test behavior, not implementation - foundational principle from Kent Beck
- "Test until fear turns to boredom" - stop when you've covered important cases
- 100% coverage is not a goal - risk-based coverage is more effective
- Property-based testing provides comprehensive coverage with fewer tests
- Over-mocking is one of the most common anti-patterns
- Tests must earn their keep - value must exceed maintenance cost
